What the app does, and what it does not promise

DualDash reads performance counters and time zone data that Windows provides, and displays them. The readings are only as accurate as what the operating system and your hardware report:

  • Temperature readings depend on sensors your hardware exposes. Many machines expose none, and DualDash says so plainly rather than inventing a number.
  • GPU, disk and network figures come from Windows performance counters and may differ from other tools that measure over different intervals or count different engines.
  • Alarms rely on your computer being awake and the app running. DualDash cannot wake a sleeping or powered-off machine, and should not be relied on as the only alarm for anything critical.
